# 📊 PRISM INSURANCE PVT. LTD. – Power BI Dashboard

## 🔍 Project Overview
This Power BI project analyzes the business performance of **PRISM Insurance Pvt. Ltd.**, focusing on key metrics such as **sales performance**, **claims analysis**, and **agent productivity**.  
The goal is to provide actionable insights for better decision-making in the insurance domain.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Track **monthly revenue** and **policy sales growth**
- Analyze **claim trends** by type and status
- Identify **top-performing agents and regions**
- Monitor **customer segmentation** and retention
- Build an **interactive dashboard** for executives

---

## 🧠 Key Insights
- 📈 Sales increased steadily across North & West regions  
- 💰 Highest claim volume from Health & Vehicle policies  
- 🏆 Top 10 agents contribute over 60% of total premium revenue  
- ⏱️ Reduced claim settlement time trend identified post-Q2
